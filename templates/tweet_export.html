Step 1 — Download your archive from Twitter/X

  1. Go to Settings & Support → Settings
  2. Click Your Account → Download an archive of your data
  3. Confirm your identity and click Request archive
  4. Wait for the email (up to 24 hours), then download the .zip file
Your data never leaves your server — the ZIP is processed in memory and discarded.
